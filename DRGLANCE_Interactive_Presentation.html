Clinical Imperative & Systems Dilemma

The Asymptomatic Screening Gap & Edge Bottlenecks

Why traditional clinical workflows and naive web ML architectures fail in real-world triage.

1. The Clinical Dilemma

  • Asymptomatic Onset: Glaucoma, cataract, and periodontal caries progress without early warning pain.
  • High Specialist Barrier: Over 70% of non-urban populations lack access to certified ophthalmologists & dentists.
  • Permanent Damage: By the time a patient books a clinic visit, tissue loss is irreversible.

2. The Latency Bottleneck

  • Heavy 4K Payloads: Smartphone cameras capture 3MB–8MB photos. Uploading over mobile cellular takes 2.5s+.
  • Cold Start Spikes: Cloud serverless containers introduce 50s+ spin-downs during urgent triage checks.
  • Bandwidth Waste: Sending multi-megabyte raw pixels to cloud GPUs throttles edge devices.

3. The Black-Box Trap

  • Opaque Softmax: Standard models output 'Glaucoma: 88%' without explaining morphological basis.
  • Regulatory Rejection: FDA/CE medical AI guidelines mandate algorithmic interpretability.
  • False Negatives: Textbook 0.50 threshold fails on imbalanced medical datasets.

Interpretability & Safety

Layer-Wise Grad-CAM Saliency Attribution

De-black-boxing convolutional decision boundaries through target class gradient backpropagation.

Mathematical Formulation

// 1. Feature Map Gradients w.r.t Target Class y^c:
∂y^c / ∂A_{i,j}^k at layer 'conv2d_deep_features.18'

// 2. Global-Average Pooling (Importance Weight α_k^c):
α_k^c = (1 / Z) * Σ_i Σ_j (∂y^c / ∂A_{i,j}^k)

// 3. Rectified Weighted Combination:
L_{Grad-CAM}^c = ReLU( Σ_k α_k^c * A^k )

Interpolation: Bilinear upsampling of 7x7 activation map back to original 224x224 retinal/oral coordinates with colormap blending.

Trilogy Technical Grill Defense

  • Q: Why not run full inference on the client?
    A: Balancing initial bundle weight (<800KB) vs model weights (~14MB). Client canvas handles downsampling; backend executes SIMD inference.
  • Q: How did you prevent dataset overfitting?
    A: Applied 5-fold stratified patient-level splitting with heavy CLAHE jitter and random affine transforms.
  • Q: Why SQLite WAL over PostgreSQL for prototype?
    A: Eliminates network hops and serialization latency, providing microsecond local reads with zero concurrency locking.
